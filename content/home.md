A garage door that won't open is usually one of two things: a **broken spring** or a problem with the **opener, remote, or sensors**. They look alike from the driver's seat, but the fixes, the safety risks, and the costs are very different.

These guides help you work out which problem you have, do the few safe checks a homeowner can do, get the car out if you're stuck, and find a repair company without getting burned by a bait-and-switch quote. Each page links to its sources, and says plainly when a price or detail needs a phone call to confirm.

*General information only, not professional repair advice. Garage door springs are under extreme tension and can cause serious injury, so when in doubt, call a qualified technician. Prices vary by location.*
